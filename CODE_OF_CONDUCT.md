# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being respectful, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers may clarify, moderate, or remove contributions that violate this code. They will handle reports as confidentially as practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to **codysa90@gmail.com**. Include the relevant link or context and any requested follow-up method. Do not include secrets or private data that is not necessary to assess the report.
