4. Delivery
neutral tone (NOT announcer voice)
no dramatic acting

5. TARGET METRICS (important)
Parameter	Target
Duration	60–120 sec
RMS loudness	-18 to -12 dB
Peak	below -3 dB
Silence padding	< 300 ms

Failure modes (why most clones suck)

❌ 1. Overacting
Mechanism: model encodes exaggerated prosody → sounds fake everywhere
Fix: 80% neutral, 20% variation

❌ 2. Inconsistent mic distance
Mechanism: amplitude + EQ shifts → unstable embedding
Result: “phasey” or drifting voice
Fix: Keep your head still!

❌ 3. Too short (<30 sec)
Mechanism: insufficient phoneme coverage
Result: robotic vowels, broken consonants

❌ 4. Background noise
Mechanism: noise becomes part of identity vector
Result: permanent hiss / room tone baked into voice

❌ 5. Clipping
Mechanism: harmonic distortion → irreversible
Result: harsh, crunchy output
