## Feedback

- What was confusing?
- What failed, and how can it be reproduced?
- What required documentation?
- Which controls or features were hard to find?
- How did the generated audio sound (intelligibility, voice match, transitions,
  pauses, volume, artifacts)?
- What would prevent regular use?
- What was unexpectedly useful?
- Would you recommend it to another user? Why or why not?

## Severity

- **Critical:** installation fundamentally fails, primary rendering is broken,
  data is lost, or a significant security issue exists.
- **High:** a major workflow is unusable or repeatedly crashes.
- **Medium:** usability or reliability issue with an acceptable workaround.
- **Low:** cosmetic issue or minor inconvenience.

Resolve critical and high issues before widening distribution. Cosmetic reports
can be prioritized without blocking the entire alpha.
